# Script and voiceover timing (Version A)

## Source
Supplied script, unchanged in wording. Only punctuation was adjusted for natural pauses.

> A customer forwards you a reply from your screening tool. The AI has been messaging candidates for three weeks… and you can't explain what it said. You open the logs and can't tell where it should have stopped, and a person should have started.
>
> On Wanderly, the nurse staffing marketplace, the AI screens candidates, and then a human recruiter takes over, in that same thread. Every AI step we build ends in a handoff to a person, in the conversation it started in.
>
> The recruiter picks up mid-thread, with everything the AI said. You read one conversation, instead of piecing it together from logs.

## Duration analysis
- 107 words.
- At a calm B2B pace (about 145–150 wpm) with breath pauses, the spoken length is **about 40–43 s**.
- The full script cannot fit a 15–20 s cut and stay intelligible: that would need roughly 320 wpm. So the master is a **44 s full-script edit**. A shorter cut is proposed below and needs your approval.
