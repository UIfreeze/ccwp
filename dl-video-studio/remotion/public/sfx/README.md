Put the licensed Artlist sound files here, named by their Artlist ID: for example `91962.wav` (also accepts .aac, .m4a or .mp3).
An optional music bed goes here as `music.mp3`/`music.wav`. These files are git-ignored: licensed audio must not be committed.
The full list, with links, is in ../../../docs/SFX_CUE_SHEET.md.
